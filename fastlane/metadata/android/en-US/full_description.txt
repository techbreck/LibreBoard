LibreBoard is a fully free and open-source Android keyboard for accurate, private, offline typing. It is based on HeliBoard and Android's LatinIME, with a LibreBoard typing engine built for local correction, prediction, and swipe decoding.

LibreBoard does not request INTERNET or ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE. It has no account system, telemetry, advertising identifier, cloud typing service, or proprietary gesture library. Typed text, gesture paths, learned words, rejection history, and clipboard contents stay on the device.

Features include:

<ul>
  <li>Fused spatial, dictionary, context, and personal candidate ranking</li>
  <li>Fully open swipe typing with a geometric fallback when neural inference is unavailable</li>
  <li>Simultaneous English and German typing with a per-word language lock</li>
  <li>Local next-word and short-phrase prediction</li>
  <li>A raw-word suggestion that keeps the exact typed text one tap away</li>
  <li>Local learning and rejection memory that can be exported or deleted explicitly</li>
  <li>Searchable, pinnable, expiring clipboard history in credential-encrypted storage</li>
  <li>Number row, spacebar cursor control, swipe-delete, D-pad, split and one-handed layouts</li>
  <li>Material You, custom, and night themes plus local emoji search</li>
  <li>Direct Boot typing without exposing personal data before first unlock</li>
</ul>

An optional, separately installed English/German context-model pack improves ranking without adding network access. Missing, disabled, slow, or incompatible models fall back to the classic tap and geometric swipe engines.

Password, PIN, email, URI, no-personalized-learning, and no-suggestions fields use restrictive policies that disable context access, clipboard capture, and learning as appropriate. Incognito mode clears in-memory context and suspends suggestions, capture, and personalization until it is turned off.
